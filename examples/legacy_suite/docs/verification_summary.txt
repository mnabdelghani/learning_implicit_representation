PHYSICAL LIMIT OF THE AIRFOIL DEMONSTRATION
This is a new two-dimensional incompressible, immersed-body computational
prototype with a wake BODY FORCE and a weighted wake-velocity output. It
does not implement surface jets or report lift/drag improvement. The nominal
Reynolds number is 100; the coarse grid is 128 x 64 before spectral cutoff.
Projection yields divergence RMS below 1e-14, but masked-body speed RMS is
about 0.19. Accurate wall enforcement and spatial, temporal, domain, mask,
and penalization convergence have not been established.

The continuous fixed-parameter differential identity holds within the
constant-gain regimes. The scheduled actuator-gain jump introduces a
parameter-change term; zero incompatibility is not claimed across it.
The directional partial-action metric is not an operator norm and includes
known damping; input-block error and parameter plots provide needed context.
Conditional residuals use newly observed midpoint fields before updating;
they are not forecasts of a fully withheld future flow state.
