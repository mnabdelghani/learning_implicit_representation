ACTIVE-FLOW-CONTROL REFERENCES
Primary records checked 7 November 2026.

1. Garcia et al. (2025), International Journal of Heat and Fluid Flow 116,
   article 109913. DOI: 10.1016/j.ijheatfluidflow.2025.109913.
   https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijheatfluidflow.2025.109913
   Author manuscript: https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.16993
   Supports the physical motivation for closed-loop flow control around a
   two-dimensional NACA 0012 airfoil using blowing/suction at the surface.
   Their Re=3000 finite-element CFD/DRL study is a distinct experiment. Its
   performance numbers are not reproduced or used as results in this package.

2. Montala et al. (2025), arXiv:2509.10195, version 1.
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.10195
   Supports a separate three-dimensional wing-control reference at Re=1000
   and 20-degree angle of attack. It is a preprint; no later publication is
   assumed. A two-dimensional prototype cannot validate three-dimensional
   separation or turbulent-flow claims.

3. Angot, Bruneau, and Fabrie (1999), Numerische Mathematik 81, 497-520.
   DOI: 10.1007/s002110050401.
   https://doi.org/10.1007/s002110050401
   Author PDF: https://www.math.u-bordeaux.fr/~chabrune/publi/ABF-NM.pdf
   Supports obstacle penalization in incompressible viscous flow. The cited
   paper's analysis does not by itself verify our grid, smoothing, splitting,
   obstacle resolution, or force estimates.

4. Chorin (1968), Mathematics of Computation 22(104), 745-762.
   DOI: 10.1090/S0025-5718-1968-0242392-2.
   https://doi.org/10.1090/S0025-5718-1968-0242392-2
   Author PDF: https://math.berkeley.edu/~chorin/chorin68.pdf
   Supports the projection approach for incompressible velocity/pressure
   evolution. The packaged implementation is not a reproduction of Chorin's
   original finite-difference experiments.

5. Jacobs, Ward, and Pinkerton (1933), NACA Technical Report 460.
   https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/19930091108
   Supports the NACA four-digit airfoil family and its geometric background.
   The low-resolution immersed profile in this package is a numerical
   approximation. The report's measured aerodynamic curves are not used as
   validation data here.

The BibTeX keys are Garcia2025Airfoil, Montala2025Wing,
Angot1999Penalization, Chorin1968Projection, and Jacobs1933NACA.

These are literature references, not implemented DRL baselines. The code's
implemented comparison methods are listed in its configuration and outputs.
