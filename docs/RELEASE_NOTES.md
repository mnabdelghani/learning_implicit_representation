# Supplementary research package (prepublication)

The current local revision includes three direct-field main studies: six independent harmonic entries for Fourier prediction, all 24 entries for contact regulation, and five entries per spatial site for Burgers control. Portable code, protocols, audits, and reproduction instructions are under `experiments/direct_submission/`; [focused Supplement S2](../supplement/supplement.pdf) records sensing assumptions, all specified seeds, failures, conditioning, and refinement results. The main and focused supplement use the same direct-study figure assets and evidence identities.

The focused supplement has three sections: S1 guide, S2 current direct-study protocols, and S3 time derivatives and whole-trajectory variations. The [technical archive](../supplement/technical_archive.pdf) retains the preceding S1–S20 document with its numbering, supporting constructions, and historical examples. This separation changes document organization without changing experiments, records, or numerical results.

This is a local accompanying package, not a published release. No public raw-data download, acceptance, peer review, DOI, or software license is asserted. The author has not selected an open-source license. Full raw archives accompany their manifests separately; compact plotting datasets and source bundles do not replace complete raw records.

**Excluded:** the definitive manuscript source/PDF, alternative manuscripts, private reviews, and patches that could reconstruct withheld manuscript material.

The earlier bounded and normalized periodic Fourier experiments and coefficient-based contact/Burgers protocols remain historical supporting studies in the technical archive. Their original algorithms, numbers, and provenance are preserved; they are not relabeled as direct partial learning. Technical Archive S18 inventories those implementations. The current Fourier graph is an explicit changed physical benchmark, with actual input/partial timescale evidence and a new-input prediction episode.

The original full 100-run archive remains absent. The 100 structured and 20 paired-transfer protocols were separately regenerated and audited; those records are not recovered historical archives. Their metadata and the retained normalized-study metadata remain separate from the current direct-study package.

The active-flow example remains an unvalidated numerical prototype. Its enlarged vorticity and streamline views remain Figures S8 and S9 in the technical archive, alongside the retained Acrobot example. The archive's unanchored Fourier figure is a checked reconstruction; its historical predecessor and the untraceable empirical ellipse plot are excluded. The archive's ellipse discussion is analytic.
